# 📌 Project Overview

Modern enterprises generate millions of financial transactions every month through SAP ERP systems. While SAP S/4HANA captures these transactions efficiently, executives often struggle to gain meaningful business insights from raw financial data.

This project demonstrates how an AI Business Analyst can transform SAP Finance data into executive dashboards that support strategic decision-making through advanced analytics and AI-powered recommendations.

---

# 💼 Business Problem

Finance departments often face challenges such as:

- Slow financial reporting
- Manual KPI tracking
- Poor visibility into cash flow
- Delayed profitability analysis
- Budget overruns
- High operational costs
- Delayed payment collections
- Limited predictive financial insights

Business leaders require real-time financial intelligence rather than static monthly reports.
